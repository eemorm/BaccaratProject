Croupier

You awake in a cold dark room. Looking around, you see a man, dressed in a costume? Hard to see. He beckons you forward. "Come, come, play my game. We have a lot to talk about." You slowly march forward as you grasp the table. You take a seat and he hands you a stack of chips. The table reads "Banker     Tie     Player". The man exclaims, "Let the games begin..."
